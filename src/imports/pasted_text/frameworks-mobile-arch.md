ARCHITECTURAL RULES
--------------------------------------------------

1. Preserve the system. Adapt the interaction.

2. Mobile Atlas remains spatial.

3. Do not convert Atlas into stacked navigation screens.

4. Do not force different Atlas systems into identical templates.

5. Reuse interaction grammar where the meaning is the same.

6. Allow composition to differ where the information architecture differs.

7. EXPLORE is a semantic depth transition, not a generic CTA.

8. Touch replaces hover.

9. Spatial context should recede as content requires more reading space, not disappear arbitrarily.

10. Evidence/canvas inspection is temporary and reversible.

11. Back navigation must preserve spatial and content context.

12. Do not create new destinations or concepts that do not already exist in Atlas.

13. Use real existing content and assets wherever content is shown.

14. Keep the desktop experience protected.
